03 · The idea

One master to run the masters.

WĀHA doesn't replace a single system — it federates all of them. A Unified Protocol Translator speaks every vendor dialect through plug-in adaptors, and normalises everything into one data structure.

Adaptors

BACnet, Modbus, ONVIF, OPC-UA, MQTT, proprietary REST — one adaptor per dialect, hot-pluggable.

Unified data model

Every asset, alarm and telemetry point lands in one schema. New systems integrate in weeks, not quarters.

Control plane

Commands flow back down the same path — dim a zone, open a valve, page a PAVA zone. Two-way, audited.

04.3 · Delivery, security & resilience

Ship weekly. Fail safely. Prove everything.

A park that never closes can't take a maintenance window. Changes reach production through one automated, signed, policy-gated path — and the platform is engineered to degrade gracefully rather than go dark.

Continuous delivery — commit to park in under an hour
01
Commit
Signed commits, trunk-based, IaC and app in Git — Git is the only source of truth.
02
Build & test
Reproducible container build, unit + contract tests, SBOM generated (CycloneDX).
03
Scan & sign
Trivy/Grype CVE scan, secret scan, image signed with Sigstore cosign.
04
Policy gate
Kyverno/OPA admission — unsigned, unscanned or non-compliant images never land.
05
Twin staging
Deployed against replayed field traffic — the digital twin is the test rig.
06
Canary
Argo Rollouts to one district first, SLO-watched, auto-rollback on breach.
07
GitOps prod
Argo CD reconciles the cluster to Git. Rollback is a revert, not a rescue.

Field devices follow the same discipline through staged firmware campaigns — 04.5.

🔐 Zero trust, top to bottom

  • SPIFFE/SPIRE workload identity — every service proves who it is, mTLS on every hop
  • Keycloak OIDC + RBAC by department; no shared operator accounts
  • Secrets in OpenBao, short-lived and auto-rotated — none in config or images
  • Every control command authorised, attributed and written to an immutable audit log
  • Dangerous commands need dry-run simulation, blast-radius limits and dual approval

🧱 Resilience by construction

  • Active–active control room across two in-Kingdom sites; either can run alone
  • District edge clusters keep sensing, buffering and running local rules for 72 h offline
  • No shared fate: one adaptor crash-looping cannot take the command bus with it
  • Event-sourced streams — after any outage the park state rebuilds by replay
  • Quarterly game days with Litmus chaos experiments and full DR restore drills

🛰️ Operating the platform

  • SLOs and error budgets per service, watched by the same Grafana stack
  • Progressive delivery gates on real signals — alert latency, command success, twin lag
  • Capacity autoscaled on stream lag (KEDA), not on guesswork
  • Backups: Velero for cluster state, PITR for the data plane, restore-tested monthly

📜 Assurance & compliance

  • Aligned to NCA ECC and OTCC controls, and to IEC 62443 zones & conduits
  • PDPL-aligned: crowd analytics count, they don't identify; data stays in-Kingdom
  • SBOM + VEX per release — a CVE question is answered in minutes, not weeks
  • Full change lineage: any action traceable to a commit, an approver and an operator

05 · AI operations

Trivial faults fix themselves. People go where they matter.

Watch the triage line: green faults are auto-remediated by the platform — restarts, re-registrations, schedule nudges. Only the amber ones become work orders, dispatched to the best-placed crew.

Auto-remediation

~60–70% of faults are trivial. WĀHA clears them in seconds, no human in the loop.

AI dispatch

Skills, zone, current load and travel time decide who gets the job — not whoever answers the radio.

Shift optimisation

Workforce plans rebuilt nightly from tomorrow's events, weather and asset risk.

06 · Root-cause intelligence

Ten alarms. One cause. Found in seconds.

WĀHA's AI reads every system's logs in one place and learns the park's wiring — which upstream power, network and controller assets feed which downstream systems. When something breaks, it walks that dependency graph straight to the root.

Log intelligence

Syslogs, SNMP traps and event streams correlated in the unified data layer. Upstream ↔ downstream dependencies mapped automatically.

Instant RCA

One failed switch looks like ten failed systems. WĀHA collapses the alarm storm to the single true cause.

Right team, first time

The root cause maps to the owning department and skill set — the task lands with the right personnel, not a triage queue.

Extends to full network & asset monitoring — switches, APs, fiber, UPS — OvulScanner / SolarWinds-class visibility, inside the same pane of glass.

07 · Predictive asset health

Fix it before it breaks.

Every pump, feeder pillar, AHU and camera gets a live health score from its own telemetry. WĀHA blends three maintenance regimes into one plan:

PM

Preventive — calendar & runtime-based servicing, auto-scheduled around events.

CM

Corrective — failures become prioritised work orders with parts & SLA attached.

RM / PdM

Reliability-centred — models predict remaining useful life and pull work forward.

The scene shows a pump station drifting toward failure — and the maintenance window WĀHA books before the curve crosses the line.

08 · Crowd intelligence

See the crowd before it forms.

CCTV analytics, Wi-Fi presence and ticketing fuse into anonymised, real-time crowd statistics — density, flow and dwell — with 30-minute predictive horizons.

Surge prediction

Experience analytics

Privacy by design

09 · ESG & carbon

Every drop, watt and tonne — accounted for.

The same telemetry that runs the park reports it. WĀHA tracks resource utilisation and emissions natively, suggests the next best reduction — pump schedules, dimming curves, route consolidation — and tracks every delta it delivers.

Scope 1 · Direct

Fleet fuel, generators and refrigerants — metered at source, logged per asset.

Scope 2 · Energy

Grid electricity for lighting, pumps and buildings, tracked live against dimming and irrigation savings.

Scope 3 · Value chain

Contractors, waste streams and supply deliveries, folded in through the same data layer.

Carbon credits
